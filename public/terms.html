Terms and privacy

The full terms of use and privacy notice are still being written and will be published here before accounts open to the public. Until then, this page describes what the game actually stores.

Playing without an account

  • Every game mode works without an account.
  • Your browser keeps your settings, best scores and a random player key (for the one-a-day Daily Challenge) in its own storage. Clearing site data removes them.
  • If you post a score, the name you type and the score are shown on the leaderboard.

If you create an account

  • Stored: your email address, a one-way hash of your password (never the password), your display name, whether you’re 18 or over, and the Google or Discord account ID if you sign in that way.
  • Not stored: your date of birth. It’s checked once and only the over-18 answer is kept.
  • A single sign-in cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Only a hash of it is stored on the server.
  • Email is used only for confirming your address and resetting your password.
  • You can close your account at any time from Account. Your email, password and display name are deleted and your photos are removed from the game.

If you submit photos

See the Contributor Licence. In short: the original stays private to moderators, every copy players see has its location and camera data removed, and you can remove a photo at any time.
