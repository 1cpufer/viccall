Community Garage Contributor Licence

Version 0.1, draft. This is the working draft and hasn’t been reviewed by a lawyer yet. Text in [brackets] is still to be decided. The Community Garage doesn’t accept real uploads until it’s final.

In short

You keep your photo; VicCall gets permission to show it in the game, for free, with your name on it.

  • You keep the copyright in every photo you submit.
  • You let VicCall store, resize, crop and show it in the game, worldwide and without payment.
  • You must have taken the photo yourself and own the rights to it.
  • Every photo is reviewed before it appears, and VicCall may decline any photo.
  • Your photos never appear in a competitive game you play in.
  • You’re credited by your display name after each round that uses your photo.
  • You can remove a photo at any time; it leaves the game within [7] days.
  • VicCall won’t sell your photo or use it to train generative AI.

1. Who can contribute

You need a VicCall account and must be at least 18 to submit photos.

Playing the game never needs an account. Children under 13 can’t create an account or submit photos.

If you submit on behalf of a business or club, you confirm you’re allowed to accept this licence for it.

2. You keep ownership

Submitting a photo doesn’t transfer its copyright to VicCall. You can keep using, publishing, selling or licensing it anywhere else.

The permission you give VicCall is non-exclusive. If you later give someone else exclusive rights to a photo, remove it from VicCall first; keeping your other agreements consistent is up to you.

3. What you allow VicCall to do

When you submit a photo, you grant [Operator legal name] (“VicCall”) a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use it to run the game, as follows:

  1. Store and copy it, including in backups.
  2. Make technical changes for display: resize, crop, compress, convert the file format, adjust brightness and colour, and remove metadata.
  3. Blur or cover number plates, faces and anything else that identifies a person or a place.
  4. Show it to players as a round in any VicCall game mode, casual or competitive, on any device or platform VicCall runs on.
  5. Show it with the round’s answer, statistics and your credit after the round.
  6. Use it for moderation, quality checks and duplicate detection, including by computing a fingerprint of the image.
  7. Only if you opt in: show it, with credit, in promotional material for VicCall such as screenshots, social media posts and trailers.

VicCall’s hosting, storage and delivery providers may handle the photo only to provide these services to VicCall. VicCall won’t sell your photo, license it to anyone else, or use it to train generative AI models.

The licence can pass only to a successor that takes over running VicCall, which must honour it. It lasts until the photo is removed under section 8.

4. Credit

VicCall credits you by your display name after every round that uses your photo, and on your contributor profile.

  • Your display name can be a nickname. You can also choose to be credited as “Community photographer”.
  • The credit is never shown before players answer, because it could give the car away.
  • The general location or event appears only if you choose to show it.
  • If a credit is missing by mistake, VicCall will fix it once told; a missing credit isn’t a breach of this licence.

5. What you promise

For every photo you submit, you confirm that:

  1. You took it yourself and own all rights in it. Photos downloaded, screenshotted, or taken by someone else aren’t allowed, even with permission.
  2. No one has an exclusive licence or other claim to it that this licence would conflict with.
  3. It’s a real photograph. It isn’t generated by AI, and the car hasn’t been edited to look like something it isn’t. Ordinary editing such as cropping, exposure and colour is fine.
  4. The vehicle details you enter are your honest best knowledge.
  5. Anyone recognisable in it agreed to be photographed, or you’ve marked them for blurring.
  6. Taking and sharing it didn’t break the law or rules you agreed to, such as a car show that forbids sharing photos.

If any of these stops being true, remove the photo and tell VicCall.

6. Photo rules

The car must be the subject, and players must be able to identify it fairly. VicCall declines or restricts photos that:

  • are too blurry, dark, small or blocked to identify the car;
  • have a person, especially a child, as the main subject;
  • show a home address, house number or anything else that could locate someone’s home;
  • carry watermarks, logos or text added after the photo was taken;
  • contain nudity, violence, hate symbols or anything illegal;
  • are deliberately mislabelled or edited to mislead.

Number plates and bystanders’ faces are blurred during review. You can also blur them yourself before uploading.

7. Review and moderation

Every photo is reviewed by a person before it appears in the game. Each submission shows one of these statuses: Pending review, Needs correction, Approved, Rejected or Removed.

  • VicCall may decline, restrict or remove any photo at its discretion, including to keep quality and variety. When it does, it gives a reason where it can.
  • VicCall may correct the vehicle details, set the difficulty, and decide whether a photo is used in casual play only or in competitive play too. Every change is logged.
  • Approval doesn’t oblige VicCall to use a photo.
  • In a competitive game, no player sees a photo contributed by anyone in that game, including copies of the same photo submitted by someone else. The server enforces this.
  • VicCall may suspend your submission rights for repeated or serious breaches of these rules.

8. Removing your photos

You can remove any photo at any time from your contributions page, or by emailing [contact email]. Your permission ends for everything that happens after removal.

  • Within [7] days, the photo stops appearing in new rounds and its public links stop working.
  • Copies in backups are deleted within [30] days and aren’t used in the meantime.
  • Past scores, leaderboards and game history aren’t changed. They keep the car’s details but no longer show the photo.
  • If you opted in to promotion, no new promotional use starts after removal. Posts already published are taken down where VicCall controls them.
  • If you close your account, your photos are removed the same way.

VicCall keeps a record of each submission, your acceptance of this licence and its moderation history, without the image, for [3 years] after removal, to handle disputes and copyright complaints.

9. Complaints and copyright

Anyone can report a photo that looks wrongly identified, unsafe or infringing, using “Report this car” in the game or by emailing [contact email].

  • Copyright owners can send a takedown notice to VicCall’s designated agent: [agent name, postal address, email]. VicCall removes or restricts a disputed photo promptly while it investigates.
  • If your photo is removed because of a notice, VicCall tells you and explains how to send a counter-notice if you believe the removal was a mistake. A counter-notice, including your name and contact details, is forwarded to the person who complained, as US law requires.
  • VicCall permanently ends the submission rights of anyone who repeatedly submits photos they don’t own. [Threshold: for example, two confirmed infringements.]

10. No payment

You aren’t paid for submitting photos or for their use, even if VicCall earns money from the game, for example through ads or subscriptions. Badges, contributor ranks and profile features have no cash value and can’t be sold or exchanged.

11. Privacy

VicCall removes location, camera and other embedded data from every copy of your photo that players see.

  • Your original file is stored privately and seen only by moderators.
  • The general location or event is shown only if you choose to show it. An exact address is never shown.
  • Your email address and account details are never public.
  • How VicCall handles your personal information is set out in the [Privacy notice].

You’re responsible for the promises in section 5. If someone brings a claim against VicCall because one of them wasn’t true, you agree to cover VicCall’s reasonable costs of that claim, as far as the law allows.

  • The Community Garage is provided as it is. VicCall works to prevent misuse but can’t guarantee that no one copies a photo from the game without permission. It will help you act against copying it becomes aware of.

  • Nothing in this licence limits rights you have under consumer protection law that can’t be waived.
